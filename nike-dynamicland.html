Nike Dynamicland

Nike Dynamicland was a celebration of the launch of the new Air Max DNs collection with global simultaneously events around the world from LA, NYC, CDMX, Paris, Chengdu, Seoul and Tokyo.

Tasked with capturing the energy of the Paris event and pulling together the global content for social outputs across Nike channels.

We took a global but local approach - collaborating with local creators and hosts brings us on a journey - interviewing guests and exploring the surreal event spaces designed by Hotel Creative.
